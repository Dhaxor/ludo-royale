How do I play?

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your yard, race it around the board and into your home. Land on a rival to send it back. The full rules are in the app under Settings → How to play.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Friends on the home screen and create a room. Share the 4-letter code; your friends tap Friends → Join and enter it. The host can start at any time — computer players fill empty seats. To play on one phone, choose Pass & Play.

Who are the computer players online?

If a public table doesn't fill within a few seconds, computer players join so you never wait. They are always labelled as computer players.

I lost my connection during a match

The app reconnects automatically. If it can't, it shows a Reconnect button — your seat is held for a minute.

My purchase didn't arrive

Purchases are delivered as soon as Apple confirms them. If the app was closed during a purchase, open it again with an internet connection and the purchase is delivered automatically. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Ludo Royale doesn't use accounts, so your progress, coins and collection are saved only on your device.
