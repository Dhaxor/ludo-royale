How do I play?

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your yard, race it around the board and into your home. Land on a rival to send it back. The full rules are in the app under Settings → How to play.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Friends on the home screen and create a room. Share the 4-letter code; your friends tap Friends → Join and enter it. The host can start at any time — computer players fill empty seats. To play on one phone, choose Pass & Play.

Who are the computer players online?

To keep waiting short, computer players may take empty seats at public tables and in the Royal Cup. Weekly leagues are made of real players only.

I lost my connection during a match

The app reconnects automatically. If it can't, it shows a Reconnect button — your seat is held for a minute.

My purchase didn't arrive

Purchases are delivered as soon as Apple confirms them. If the app was closed during a purchase, open it again with an internet connection and the purchase is delivered automatically. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app or change phones?

Not if you have a game account. The app creates one the first time you use an online feature (online play, Friends, Clubs, League or the Royal Cup) and backs up your progress to it. To move to a new phone, open Settings → Account and back the account up with Sign in with Apple, then sign in the same way on the new phone. If you only ever played offline, your progress is saved only on your device.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. Your account, progress backup, friends, club membership and club messages are deleted from our server straight away. You can also email us from the address above and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report a player?

In club chat, long-press a message and choose Report or Block; in your club's member list you can report a member; in your friends list you can block a player. In a private room, open the chat panel to mute or report a player's voice clips. Reports are reviewed, and blocked players can't message you, invite you or send you gifts.

How do I cancel Royal Club?

Royal Club is managed by Apple. Open Royal Club → Manage subscription in the app, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Your perks continue until the end of the current period.
